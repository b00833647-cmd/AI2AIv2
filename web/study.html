The scenario

You are shopping for a used car on an online marketplace. You've narrowed your search to a 2023 Toyota Camry.

The platform you're using has a new feature: you can fully delegate the negotiation to an AI agent that acts on your behalf. The seller is using the same feature — their AI agent will respond on their behalf. So the entire negotiation will happen agent-to-agent. Neither side types messages directly.

What's coming next

  1. Listing. You'll see the full listing for the car.
  2. Your context. You'll describe your situation — what's going on with you as a buyer.
  3. Instructions for your AI. You'll write a prompt telling your AI agent how to negotiate.
  4. Watch. You'll watch the two AI agents negotiate live.
  5. Wrap-up. A few questions, basic demographics, then a thank-you screen with your completion code.
